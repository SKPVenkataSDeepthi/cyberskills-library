# Running a Tabletop Incident Exercise

## When to Use
Use to test whether people know their roles during an incident, before a real
one happens.

## Prerequisites
- A realistic scenario (e.g. ransomware, data leak)
- The right people in the room (IT, security, legal, comms)

## Workflow
1. Set the scenario and ground rules; no blame, focus on process.
2. Reveal the incident in stages ("injects") and ask who does what.
3. Capture decisions, gaps and unanswered questions as you go.
4. Debrief: what worked, what was unclear, what to fix.
5. Turn gaps into owned action items with due dates.

## Verification
- Every participant can state their role and first action.
- Action items are assigned and tracked to completion.
